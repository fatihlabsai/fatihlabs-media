3. Solve, Grade, Retrain: Sakana AI's MASS Experiment (50)

### Description
In Sakana AI's MASS method, a single model solves a task, grades its own work, and retrains itself on its best-scoring attempts. It improves on research tasks, though not in every area, and if it misses a mistake both while solving and while grading, it can treat that mistake as a success and reinforce it. The lesson is simple: on important work, let someone else grade you too.
